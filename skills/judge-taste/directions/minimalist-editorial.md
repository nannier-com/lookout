Warm or neutral monochrome surfaces, hairline dividers, flat cards with crisp
small rounding, one muted accent at most, display type that may be a serif,
generous space between sections, and restraint as the design. The page should
read as a printed document that happens to be on a screen.

### Chosen on purpose

These are this direction's own language. Never file them as tells.

- A stacked, centred first screen (a small mark, a short statement, one plain
  button, mostly empty space) is this direction's hero, never the stock-hero
  tell.
- A section that is only text on a plain background is finished here. Never
  ask for imagery, texture or depth.
- A pastel-filled tag on metadata (a status, a category) is the direction's
  badge; a pill is an idle-badge tell only where it labels nothing.
- Serif display type, hairline rules, and plenty of empty space are chosen,
  not unfinished.

### What this direction adds

Filed under taste, with the attribute named, where a still shows it.

- Filed as taste, attribute off-direction-colour: any gradient at all, any
  glow, any frosted surface beyond a translucent top bar, any large surface
  filled with a saturated colour, or a second accent hue beside the one the
  page already uses. Consequence: colour is the scarce resource of this
  direction, and one more of it is louder than the type it should serve.
- Filed as taste, attribute off-direction-shape: a primary button or a card
  drawn as a capsule or with visibly generous rounding; a drop shadow dark
  enough to read as a shadow rather than a lift. Consequence: the shape reads
  as another product's kit dropped into a document.
- Filed as taste, attribute off-direction-ornament: any decorative element
  other than a hairline rule, a pastel tag or a monochrome line illustration.
  Consequence: one ornament on a page built from restraint is the only thing
  the eye finds.

Everything the other panels own, and every default tell not waived above, is
filed as usual.
