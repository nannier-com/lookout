One product, shown. A near-black or pure-black ground, a single photograph or
render carrying the screen edge to edge, display type at monumental scale, one
action, and space at the scale of half a screen. Restraint is not modesty here:
the screen is largely empty so that the one image and the few words on it are
unavoidable.

### Chosen on purpose

These are this direction's own language. Never file them as tells.

- A first screen that is one image, a handful of words and one button is this
  direction's hero, never the stock-hero tell.
- Display type at several times the size of anything else, uppercase and
  letter-spaced, is the hierarchy working as intended, never a scale defect.
- A section that is an image with a short caption and nothing else is finished
  here. Never ask for supporting copy, a feature list or a diagram.
- Empty space occupying most of a screen is the composition, never an
  unfinished layout or a spacing defect.
- A near-black ground is the direction; judge contrast on the text drawn over
  the image, not on the darkness of the surface.

### What this direction adds

Filed under taste, with the attribute named, where a still shows it.

- Filed as taste, attribute off-direction-shape: a bordered card grid, a boxed
  feature list, or a rail of framed thumbnails. Consequence: the frames compete
  with the image the screen exists to show, and the product stops being the
  subject.
- Filed as taste, attribute off-direction-colour: a second accent beside the
  one the page already uses, or a colour gradient laid over a photograph.
  Consequence: the image loses the screen to a colour that means nothing.
- Filed as taste, attribute off-direction-type: paragraphs at reading length on
  an image-led screen, or body text small enough to need leaning in.
  Consequence: the screen changes register mid-scroll and becomes a brochure
  page with a large picture at the top.
- Filed as taste, attribute off-direction-ornament: badges, ribbons, pills and
  rows of small icons. Consequence: several small things ask for attention at
  once, on a screen whose whole method is asking for it once.
- Filed as taste, attribute off-direction-shadow: a drop shadow used to lift a
  surface off a near-black ground, where it reads as a smudge rather than a
  lift. Consequence: depth that does not survive the ground it is drawn on.

Everything the other panels own, and every default tell not waived above, is
filed as usual.
